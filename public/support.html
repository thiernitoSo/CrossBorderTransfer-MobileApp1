Frequently Asked Questions

  • How long does a transfer take?

    Transfer times vary based on the destination and payment method. Mobile money transfers typically arrive within minutes, while bank transfers can take 1-3 business days. Cash pickups are usually available within hours after the transfer is processed.

  • What are the transfer fees?

    Our fee structure is transparent and depends on the amount you send:

    • For amounts up to $100: $5.00 flat fee
    • For amounts $101-$500: $10.00 flat fee
    • For amounts $501-$1000: $15.00 flat fee
    • For amounts over $1000: 1.5% of the transfer amount
  • How do I add a new recipient?

    You can add a new recipient by going to the "Beneficiaries" section in your dashboard and clicking on "Add New Beneficiary". You'll need to provide their name, country, phone number, and preferred payment method details.

  • Can I cancel a transfer?

    You can cancel a transfer as long as it's still in "pending" status. Once a transfer has been processed or completed, it cannot be canceled. To cancel a pending transfer, go to the "Transactions" section, find the transaction you want to cancel, and click the "Cancel" button.

  • What payment methods can I use?

    We currently support the following payment methods:

    • Debit cards
    • Credit cards
    • Bank transfers
